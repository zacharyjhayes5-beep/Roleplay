Cold call simulator · voice

Practice the intro on somebody who talks back.

A synthetic prospect answers the phone as a ball of energy. It hears you, pushes back the way real people do, and only warms up when you actually work the script. Your lines stay on the left the whole time — the cues light up as you hit them.

01 · Pick the heat

Four prospects, from a pleasant retiree to a guy who answers with "not interested." Higher levels interrupt more, relent less, and will hang up on you.

02 · Talk, don't read

Allow the mic and just say your opener. The orb listens, and the teleprompter marks each cue the moment it hears it. No mic? Type your lines instead.

03 · Get the debrief

At hang-up you get a score per beat, every objection you faced, filler-word count, and notes on what to fix before the next dial.
